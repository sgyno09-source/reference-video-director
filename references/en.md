# English Usage Notes

## In one sentence

Turn a reference image plus a rough story idea into a directly usable director-style video prompt.

## Recommended invocation

```text
Use reference-video-director.

Character: {{Mixed 1}}
Outfit: {{Mixed 2}}
Scene: {{Mixed 3}}

Duration: 30 seconds
Model: Seedance
POV: boyfriend first-person

Story:
she pretends to be annoyed after something he says,
he is terrible at comforting her,
and accidentally makes her laugh.

Requirements:
believable everyday couple chemistry,
no idol-drama acting,
natural dialogue,
focus on micro-expressions and suppressed laughter.
```

## What creates believable chemistry

Do not rely on polished romantic lines.

Better signals include:

- half-second pauses
- a small upward glance
- pressed lips
- looking away
- sneaking a glance back
- suppressing a smile
- physically plausible touch and withdrawal
- a dry, awkward line that lands because the relationship already feels familiar

## Minimal-input behavior

If the user gives only a one-line idea, do not demand a long form.

Infer conservative defaults and keep uncertain details easy to edit.
